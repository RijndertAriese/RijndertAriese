### Hi, I'm Rijndert

I'm a research technician turned PhD candidate in the [Rios group (Dream3D Lab)](https://www.dream3dlab.com) at the Princess Máxima Center for Pediatric Oncology in Utrecht. For the past 10 years I've worked on **human organoid models**, **3D microscopy** and **single-cell transcriptomics**.

**What I work on**
- Building and characterising brain organoid models, most recently a brainstem organoid model of diffuse midline glioma
- Imaging intact tissue and organoids: tissue clearing, multispectral confocal and light-sheet microscopy
- Analysing single-cell and bulk RNA-seq: QC, annotation, integration and reference mapping to human atlases

**Selected work**
- [**BrO_DMGO_analysis**](https://github.com/Dream3DLab/BrO_DMGO_analysis): analysis code for *De novo H3.3K27M-altered diffuse midline glioma in human brainstem organoids to dissect GD2 CAR T cell function* ([Nature Cancer, 2026](https://doi.org/10.1038/s43018-025-01084-0), shared first author). Time-course scRNA-seq of brainstem organoids and engineered tumours, mapped against the HNOCA and the human developing brain atlas.
- Imaging protocols: [High-resolution 3D imaging of fixed and cleared organoids](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41596-019-0160-8) (Nat. Protoc. 2019), [mLSR-3D multispectral imaging of intact tissue](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41596-022-00739-x) (Nat. Protoc. 2022), [Single-cell resolution 3D imaging of intact organoids](https://doi.org/10.3791/60709) (JoVE 2020, shared first author)

**Tools I use**
`R` · `Python` · `bash` · `FIJI` · `Imaris` 

Currently **interested** in combining **imaging** with **single-cell and spatial omics**, and in applying these methods to **developmental** and **tumour biology**.
